About this project

I study Technical Physics at TU Wien and created extensive personal elaborations while working through exercises, preparing for examinations and revising course material.

I created this independent website to make selected materials available to other students. The documents reflect my personal understanding at the time they were written and may contain errors.

This website is not affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by TU Wien. It does not replace official course material, lectures, textbooks or information provided by the university.

Supporting the project

Preparing, reviewing and maintaining these materials requires time. If the material on this website is helpful to you in any way, please consider donating. :)

Even a small amount can help me keep this site running and continue helping other students.

If you add your name to the donation, I will also place you on the leaderboard. :)

Access to the currently published materials does not depend on making a donation.
