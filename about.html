About this project

I studied Technical Physics at TU Wien and created extensive personal elaborations while working through exercises, preparing for examinations and revising course material.

I created this independent website to make selected materials available to other students. The documents reflect my personal understanding at the time they were written and may contain errors.

This website is not affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by TU Wien. It does not replace official course material, lectures, textbooks or information provided by the university.

Supporting the project

Preparing, reviewing and maintaining these materials requires time. People who find the website helpful may support the project voluntarily.

Access to the currently published materials does not depend on making a donation.
